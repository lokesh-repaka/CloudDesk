# Handling Payment Disputes and Unauthorized Charge Inquiries

CloudDesk prioritizes security and fraud prevention for all credit card transactions.

## Deterministic Escalation Policy

Any customer inquiry alleging:
- Unauthorized credit card transactions
- Stolen corporate cards or banking fraud
- Threat of bank chargeback or dispute

**MUST BE DETERMINISTICALLY ESCALATED** directly to the Finance & Risk Engineering team. Autonomous AI agents are strictly prohibited from closing, resolving, or issuing unverified refunds for disputed charges without formal finance approval.
