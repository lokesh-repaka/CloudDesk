# Account Erasure, Workspace Deletion, and GDPR Compliance

Under GDPR Article 17 and CCPA, customers have the right to request permanent deletion of personal data and legacy workspace resources.

## Strict Policy Escalation

Permanent data destruction operations **MUST BE ESCALATED** to the **Data Privacy & Legal Operations** team.

- Autonomous AI agents must never execute automated data destruction scripts without human legal review.
- The ticket severity is classified as `high` or `critical` by policy rules.
- Once verified by legal, a 30-day graceful recovery window is provided before irreversible database sanitization.
