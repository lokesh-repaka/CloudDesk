# Seat Management and Prorated Refund Policy

CloudDesk subscriptions are billed per active seat on a monthly or annual cadence.

## Seat Reductions

When an account downgrades seat capacity mid-cycle:
- Unused capacity is calculated proportionally from the effective change date to the end of the current billing cycle.
- Credits are automatically applied toward subsequent billing invoices.

## Refund Requests

- Any cash refund or credit exceeding $0 requires human operator review via the CloudDesk Approval Gate.
- Support agents can submit a `create_refund_request` action specifying the calculated refund amount and rationale.
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
